Today, I return to doing what I love: writing.

Today, I announce my next challenge after my first 30 days of writing gave me room to grow and to learn, to express my thoughts, my world, and the reality around me.

I'm always looking to grow and gain feedback. To my knowledge, this is how you get better at a thing.

I've written many pieces up to this point, 51 to be exact, and I have no reason to stop.

My challenge for October is to complete 31 days of sharing my thoughts, opinions, and life lessons.

That's something else writing has done for me. Not only is it therapeutic, but it allows me to be open, honest, and detailed in my views. To be vulnerable and accepting.

I am still a young author, but with time, I have plans to leave a body of work that people will find. And maybe one article, one line, will give someone a different perspective or change their mind on something.

I also want to provide something for the reader, if that's their thing. And I know many of you just want to read a piece of real writing. Not picture-perfect, but something that comes from a person just like you.

If you choose to follow along, I hope you find that in my everyday drops.

There won't be a central theme. I will write about the day and what that day brought.

On December 5th, 2025, I started and dropped my first Day 1 piece. If you haven't, give it a minute, literally (smiles). You can view it here:

[Day 1 — No More Waiting, Today I Choose Action](https://medium.com/tenacious-individual-performance/day-1-begin-7df690cfe384)

Who knows what the next 30 days will hold? I very much appreciate you reading my work, and I hope something I leave makes you think, smile, or makes your day that much better.

Thank you for being here and, again, for reading.

Day 2, we open for real for real (smiles).
