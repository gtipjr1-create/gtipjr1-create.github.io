## **December 31st, 2025. New Year’s Eve.**

### **Past Notes and Reflection: New Year**

As the end of 2025 approaches, I’ve been in thought much of the day. My morning, routine. I’ve set good habits and systems to bring over to the new year and beyond. I have been continuously working on myself and improving. I overcome daily.

New goals and tweaked systems. Looking over my notes, I came across something I wrote back in either November or early December:

**All the things I want to see in my life.**

This is an extensive list, so I’ll condense it for the sake of the article and attention span. It starts like this:

> All the things I want to see: financial success, prosperity, happiness, cheerfulness, joy, laughter, politeness, healthy people, happy faces, good food, nice clothes, cleanness and neatness, easygoing people, books, new gym, new computer, structure, good habits, routines, new experiences, new money, accepting people, new shoes, myself with a clean appearance, new job, new business, become a writer, my brand, new and nice music, generosity, understanding, clarity, competence, calmness, positivity, consistency, discipline, order, and faith.

I told you it was extensive, and this is the condensed version (*smiles*).

---

I have managed to think on these things often and feel them, and they have come.

I wanted to change. I desired it deeply. I reminded myself very often that I will and I won’t give up.

I’m the type of person who is calm and confident. I’m the type of person who is focused and decisive. I’m the type of person who is committed and disciplined. I’m the type of person who takes action towards my purpose and goals.

I decide and I do. I have faith in myself. I have faith in my plans. I obey. I move. So I become.

---

> “True values and beliefs are only and always expressed in behavior.”

> “You can’t hit a target you can’t see.” — Brian Tracy

### 2026, I’m ready!
