Day 29: January 2nd, 2026

**Walking My Walk**

January 2nd, 2026 — two days in, and today is my second entry of the month.

Today (off day), I awoke at 7:20am. Why am I up? I don’t have work today. I should be resting. I did go to bed late last night — stayed up strengthening my infrastructure, my systems, and going over my planned changes for my new journeys ahead.

The first: finish getting my place in order. I picked up a 3-shelf bookshelf from Target last night. Today I put it together.

A bit skeptical at first, but after reading the instructions and a second gloss over, I pulled out the needed tools, laid out my pieces, and quickly the slabs of white-painted wood became a bookshelf (*smiles*).

When you’re first pulling the slabs out and the bags of screws and nails, you might think, “*Man, can I do this?”* And before you know it, you’ve taken the steps — slow and careful — and have built something you can be proud of.

That’s me now, all smiles, looking at my newly built bookshelf. I wasn’t timing myself, but I figured I was done in an hour and a half. I put on some focus music and got to work.

---

After all is done, I take my two 3-feet stacks and begin to organize and place them on the shelves. My small library is coming along nicely, and in a short while I’ll have every shelf covered. Well, I basically have almost two filled now, but I look forward to adding more.

I pull a meal prep container from the fridge: rice, 93/7 lean beef seasoned with rosemary, pepper, and thyme, spinach, and a fried egg on top. A quick zap. I say my grace and I dig in.

I enjoy cooking. I like to cook. I like beef with rosemary. I like red wines. I like thyme with bison. I like dark beers. I like cottage cheese and pineapple. I like almond butter smeared on Oreo cookies. I’m willing to try all sorts of things. I enjoy foods and different cultures.

Most of my diet is centered around: Mediterranean, Mexican, Indian, Chinese, and Italian.

---

Needing to run a few errands, I get dressed and head out for the day. Another rainy day in San Francisco, but the show goes on.

**My first stop: Ross.** I’m a frugal shopper. Joining a new gym, it’s a new year — I need new gym clothes. And I’ll probably grab something for work.

I’m very straightforward when I shop. I know what I want, where it is. I find it, try it on, look around a little bit, and check out. All the while I’m doing this, ***Atomic Habits*** is in my ear.

I get to the line. It’s empty — 3 checkers in front of me, all standing idle. I say nothing. Moments later, they all walk away from their registers. Odd, but I guess maybe it’s break time. Even though usually in retail, all the clerks don’t break at the same time. I shake it off.

Still standing. 1 checker to my right. She’s wrapping up with a shopper, so I’ll be next shortly.

Before the older lady could even grab her bags and her cane, the clerk — not even talking to me but talking at me (if you know what I mean) — asks, “*Do you need a bag*?” in an overdramatic tone.

“*Yes*,” trying to meet her eyes. The whole interaction was lopsided. Here I am, happy, smiling, having a wonderful day. I glance at her name tag. Let’s just call her Sam.

I wondered what was wrong, even though I shouldn’t think into this much. She rang my things up, never looked at me till she asked, “*Are you paying cash or card?*”

I replied, “*Cash*.” And she has this twist in her facial expression. “*Oh, cash,”* she says, somewhat disgusted. I shake it off.

I hand her the bill in her hand with a smile and ask, “*Is this fine*?” She runs it through her counterfeit scanner, counts out the change, and puts it away from me on the counter and gives me the change in my hand.

I think, *Wow*! Then ask, “*Can you hand me my money?*” She balls it slightly before putting it in my hand. I say, “*Thank you.*” She looks past me and calls for the next customer. “*NEXT*!”

I walk to the door, show my receipt, and leave.

---

In my mind, I ask myself, *What is happening? Did I — no, I didn’t do anything wrong*. I shake it off, turn up my audiobook, and continue on with my day.

It has started raining now. Gloomy, but still a good day.

I’ve been wanting pizza for maybe three weeks, so I say “*what the heck*” and grab me a box and head home. A walk in the rain, eating a slice, and enjoying my life.

The old Garry would have had a lot to say to that clerk. But I can’t harp on the uncontrollables. People are people, and maybe she was just having a bad day. We’re all human, so I’ll leave it at that.

---

I enjoy my walk home, get in, eat, and — well, now I’m writing this.

It’s been great having you tag along with me. One more day till I complete my 30-day writing challenge.

One of the most important things I’m continuing to work on — and it’s a continuous process — is to not revert back to old ways of thinking. I am new. I think better. I am compassionate and understanding. I choose to see the best. I want the best for others I don’t even know. I want change.

### **If it’s hard, do it hard.If it’s easy, do it easy.**
