I stand on it.

I stand tall.

Moving forward, I can’t please everyone. I also know I’m not for everyone. I cannot help everyone like I thought I could.

You have to want some things for yourself — including change. I can’t give a person desire. But I can and will tend to mine.

And by me doing that, if I offend people, so be it. Life is too short to be small. And I’m a giant.

- Upward and onward to better and greater things. But in it all were lessons. Lessons that brought me to even this point of writing, and now reciting, this reflection.
- To learn.
- It has all been a learning experience. No matter how harsh. Truly, what doesn’t kill you makes you stronger. And wiser.
- But it is up to you to use the experience and apply the knowledge and wisdom.
