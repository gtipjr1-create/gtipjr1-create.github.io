Saturday, October 3rd.

You ever have one of those days where you sleep in, wake up refreshed, you're smiling, and you just feel good?

You do the small things around the house, and you don't mind. You put on some music, lock in, and just vibe.

A flood of memories, some good, some bad. From track to track, almost every song has a little bit of history attached.

You let the feelings flow, enjoying times from way back.

Another song, another scene. You can see it so vividly.

A time machine. How marvelous the mind is.

So, have you ever had one of those days?

Thank you for being here and reading.

Today was a lazy day.
