## January 3rd, 2026

**I Started, I Wrote, and I’ve Changed**

Saturday. I’m up early, still in bed I lay. I think how thankful I am for my bed and the many, many things I have now — small and large. I’m thankful for rest, healing, and great sleep. I’m thankful for food and for drink. I’m thankful for discipline, consistency, and persistence.

I’m thankful for change (*smiles*).

I get up, reach for my water and notebook. I have a few things to do today, nothing pressing, so I’ll pace myself.

First, I need to knock out laundry. This pop-up hamper with handles is awesome. I toss a few more things from the floor, my notebook, and ***Dear Writer*** — I can read while I wait. I put on my walking shoes and my coat, and I’m out the door.

---

San Francisco has been rainy but bright. We have microclimates here. I can see down the hill from where I’m standing — no rain, just sunlight. Getting out early, getting this done also doubles as my cardio.

Up the hills I go. My hamstrings and glutes feel every step as I toss the hamper into my other hand as if I’m holding a bride to the room. I march, thinking about my goals and how I’m grateful things are finally good.

Almost forgot — passing a small shop, the wise lady here is always really nice to me. I grab some Tide and a protein shake since I left without eating. I get to the counter and I’m greeted with, “*How are you, sir?*” A smile, gentle and sincere.

I smile back and answer, “*Well*! On my way to the laundromat. *How is your morning*?”

“*Good*.”

I pay for my things, and as I’m leaving she says, “*Have a good day, sir.*”

(*Smiles*.)

As I continue my walk, I pass a lady. We meet eyes and exchange our smiles. She nods and I nod. Happy. I wish more people were like that.

---

Making it in, the place is quiet. I think maybe a customer or 2. I load my things, take a seat, and dive into my reading. I’m learning and enjoying the lessons Maggie has filled this book with — all sorts of help. Pen in hand, ever so often I take notes.

Before I know it, the machine has stopped. *Wow, was I reading for 40 minutes? I guess I was*.

I toss my things into the dryer, hit medium heat, and go back to continue. I stop after 4 chapters — I don’t want to finish too soon. I turn on Mr. Hill. I downloaded a video from the **Master Key Society**: *Lessons and Laws*. I listen deeply, focusing and hearing clearly.

After a short while, I pull my things from the dryer and move them over to the counter so I can fold and think.

---

After taking my clothes home, one maybe two more errands. I get those done and come back home.

I have a new cast iron skillet I need to season. This is my first time, and it went well.

Meal prep will consist of sirloin, bells and onions, jasmine rice for my carbs. Against the grain I cut, turning my steak into strips. I’ve charred my veggies, now to flash fry these 4 batches and combine.

Everything came out wonderful. I should have bought a cast iron sooner, but now that I own one, I’ll take care of it. This should last me a lifetime.

I fill my containers. The skillet I wash with water and dry on the stove. I add a drop of oil, rub it in. Now my cleanup is done.

To my bowl of rice, I add the steak and veggie mixture, topping it with avocado, fresca, and sour cream. I say my grace and — what a treat. The steak is perfect, and the char on the bells is just how I like. I’m turning out to be a not-so-bad cook (*smiles*).

I say my grace and dig in.

---

While eating, I can’t help but think: ***Today is my 30th day.***

I’ve done it. I’ve challenged myself to write for 30 consecutive days. Nobody made me do this. This is something that in my mind, in my heart, I knew I needed and wanted to do. And when I started, I told myself, “*I’m just going to do i*t.” Unplanned. I believed in myself, and I told myself, *“Just do it*!”

And well, I’ve been doing it.

I’m, to my knowledge, a writer. I’m an author. I am consistent. I’m learning, and I promise I’ll get better.

---

In all, and I haven’t spoken on this till now: **I, in fact, have been journaling consecutively since October 30th, 2025, to this very moment.**

I, in fact, have done what I’ve written. I’ve documented my changing. I have the proof. Literally, **I am the proof.**

My writing won’t stop here. Though my challenge is complete, I’m working on something big, and I can’t wait to showcase it to you guys.

---

Thank you for taking time to read into my journey. I’m thankful and grateful for you.

I have learned so much about myself. Still learning, always learning. I’ve been diligent, disciplined, consistent. I’ve been persistent. I persevere. I believed every step of the way that I’ll get better.

> I did. I do.

*For we walk by faith and not by sight*.

> “Lord my God, I cried to You for help, and You restored my health.” — Psalm 30:2
