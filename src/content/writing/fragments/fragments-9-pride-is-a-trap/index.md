#### On unlearning what once defined you

Pride isn’t always a good thing.

Sometimes, it’s the reason you stay the same.

I’ve been proud of things that were actually holding me back.

Proud of how much I could endure.

Proud of doing everything alone.

Proud of pushing through without stopping.

For a while, that worked.

Until it didn’t.

⸻

Because the things I was proud of…

became the things I refused to question.

That’s the trap.

You don’t outgrow what you’re still protecting.

⸻

I had to unlearn the idea that struggle equals progress.

I had to let go of the identity that said:

If it’s hard, it must be right.

That mindset built something —

but it also limited it.

It kept me in cycles of effort without direction.

⸻

Unlearning that wasn’t comfortable.

It felt like stepping away from proof.

Like letting go of something I had earned.

But holding onto it would have cost me more.

⸻

Now, I don’t measure myself by how much I can endure.

I measure by how well I can build.

Structure over chaos.

Clarity over intensity.

Progress over pride.

⸻

Not everything you’re proud of deserves to come with you.
