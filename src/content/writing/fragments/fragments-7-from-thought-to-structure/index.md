#### On preserving what matters

Thought, by nature, is unstable.

It moves with attention, shifts with emotion, and often disappears before it is fully understood. What feels clear in one moment can be gone in the next.

Structure is what preserves it.

I am learning to shape ideas, to place them somewhere they can be returned to, and to give them a form that allows them to last.

Without structure, thought is temporary. With it, it becomes something that can endure.
