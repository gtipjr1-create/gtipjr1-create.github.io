#### On structure, clarity, and directed energy

Lately, I have been learning that progress is not only about effort. It is also about order.

A person can be willing, serious, and ready to work, yet still feel scattered when too many things are being carried at once. The answer is not always more force. Sometimes the answer is better arrangement.

I am learning to order my effort. To close what is open, place things where they belong, and move with more structure and less internal noise.

Order does not reduce ambition. It strengthens it.
