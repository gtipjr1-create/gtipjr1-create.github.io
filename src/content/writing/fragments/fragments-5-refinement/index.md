Simple doesn’t mean easy.

Not a simpleton, but I like simplicity.

Faith is not just something that falls from the sky. It’s built by repetition, belief, and action — toward the vision.

I am a man of purpose: action, faith, wisdom, courage, and discipline. Consistency, persistence, and perseverance. Love, kindness, optimism, positive mental attitude, and integrity rounds out my character.

Refinement is what I’m going through in this stage in my life.

Always getting better. Stronger. Physically, mentally, emotionally.

The fire (pain) is removing all the things that are not meant for my elevation.

I am ready. I know the lessons in this season I must overcome, and I will.

I have to clean up the rooms. I will.

I am responsible. It is up to me and only me to do the work.

I recognize it. I am aware.

I ask for more courage and wisdom to control my mind, to do the things I need to do. To continue to let go of old ways. To continue the purge of limited beliefs.

I do not want them, and they are not welcome here.

Fragments continue.
