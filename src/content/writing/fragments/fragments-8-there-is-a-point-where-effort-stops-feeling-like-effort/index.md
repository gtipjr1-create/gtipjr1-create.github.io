#### Proof through repetition

I remember when this was just something I said.

Something I believed — but hadn’t proven yet.

Now I don’t have to say it the same way.

I can see it.

I’ve been showing up.

Every day.

Not perfect — but consistent.

I built the apps.

Forge. SelfTrainer. FitPulse.

Not ideas — real work.

I finished the book.

Published it.

I kept training.

Long enough to actually see the change.

That matters.

Because there were days where nothing showed.

Days where it felt slow.

Days where it would’ve been easy to fall off.

I didn’t.

The discipline I once had to force

now carries itself.

I stayed aligned with the vision.

I stayed patient.

I stayed grounded.

And now things are moving.

Not all at once.

But enough to know I’m on the right path.

I meant it when I said I would become this.

Now I’m becoming it.

And it continues.

Still here.

Still building.

Fragments continue.
